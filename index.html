What is Top Down Analysis?

A professional trading methodology that starts from the highest timeframe (Monthly/Weekly) to identify the primary trend, then drills down to daily and intraday charts to find high-probability entries with proper structure and risk management.

1. HTF Trend
Identify the dominant market direction on higher timeframes.
2. MTF Zones
Mark key support/resistance and order blocks.
3. LTF Entry
