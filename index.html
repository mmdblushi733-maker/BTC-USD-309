Top Down Analysis — The Framework

Understand how professional traders structure their analysis before placing any trade.

🌐
Step 01 — Higher Timeframe (HTF)

Identify the Macro Trend

Start on the Weekly or Daily chart. Determine the dominant direction using EMA 200 and market structure (HH/HL for uptrend, LH/LL for downtrend). This sets the overall bias — only trade in this direction.

🔍
Step 02 — Mid Timeframe (MTF)

Locate Key Supply & Demand Zones

Drop to the H4 chart. Identify institutional order blocks, fair value gaps, and liquidity pools. These are your areas of interest — where price is likely to react or reverse.

🎯
Step 03 — Lower Timeframe (LTF)

Precision Entry & Confirmation

On the H1 or M15 chart, wait for a confirmation pattern at the MTF zone: CHoCH (Change of Character), BOS (Break of Structure), or an engulfing candle. Never enter blind — let price confirm before risking capital.

Common Mistakes to Avoid
❌

Trading Against the HTF Trend

Taking sell signals when the Daily and Weekly chart are bullish is one of the fastest ways to get stopped out. Always trade in the direction of the higher timeframe bias.

❌

Ignoring Market Structure

Entering trades at random price levels without considering structure (HH/HL, LH/LL) means you're guessing. Structure is the map — every trade should have a structural reason.

❌

No Risk Management Plan

Even the best analysis fails sometimes. Without a hard stop loss and defined risk-reward ratio, one losing trade can wipe weeks of gains. Always define your risk before entry.

❌

Forcing Trades in Ranging Markets

When ATR is low and price moves sideways, the market is in consolidation. Trading inside a range without clear direction leads to repeated stop-outs. Learn to wait for breakouts or volume expansion.
